## G1 — Core Evidence Register

### ER-01 CDC Program Evaluation Framework, 2024
Kidder, D. P., Fierro, L. A., Luna, E., et al. (2024). CDC Program Evaluation Framework, 2024. MMWR Recommendations and Reports, 73(6), 1–37. https://doi.org/10.15585/mmwr.rr7306a1
Use: principal evaluation-process framework.
Verified constructs: six steps (Assess context; Describe the program; Focus evaluation questions and design; Gather credible evidence; Generate and support conclusions; Act on findings); three cross-cutting actions; five evaluation standards.
Claim control: use current 2024 names, not legacy 1999 labels unless explicitly comparing versions.

### ER-02 CDC Program Evaluation Framework Action Guide, 2024
Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. (2024). CDC Program Evaluation Framework Action Guide.
Use: practical application for novice/early-career evaluators; distinction among evaluation, surveillance, research, and monitoring; application of standards/cross-cutting actions.

### ER-03 WHO & UNESCO Global Standards and Indicators, 2021
World Health Organization & United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization. (2021). Making every school a health-promoting school: Global standards and indicators. ISBN 978-92-4-002505-9.
Use: school-health systems context, standards and indicators.
Evidence note: based on review of policies/strategies/guidelines from 91 countries plus expert/public consultation.

### ER-04 WHO Guideline on School Health Services, 2021
World Health Organization. (2021). WHO guideline on school health services. ISBN 978-92-4-002939-2.
Use: definition and evidence-based context of school health services and health needs of school-age children/adolescents.

### ER-05 WHO/UNESCO/UNICEF/WFP Implementation Guidance, 2021
World Health Organization, UNESCO, UNICEF, & World Food Programme. (2021). Making every school a health-promoting school: Implementation guidance. ISBN 978-92-4-002507-3.
Use: implementation barriers/enablers, adaptation and implementation of HPS global standards.

### ER-06 WHO SEARO Roadmap, 2022
World Health Organization Regional Office for South-East Asia. (2022). Roadmap for implementation of health promoting schools in the South-East Asia Region. ISBN 9789290209799.
Use: regional implementation context, key actions, timeframes, indicators, intersectoral health–education collaboration.

### ER-07 WHO School Health Services Implementation Guidance, 2026
World Health Organization. (2026). Making every school a health-promoting school: Implementation guidance for school health services. ISBN 978-92-4-011896-6.
Use: current practical school health services implementation; eleven priority areas, planning tools/checklists, whole-school approach.
Freshness note: use as current implementation guidance while distinguishing it from the 2021 normative guideline.

### ER-08 WHO Guideline Web Annex A Compendium, 2021
World Health Organization. (2021). WHO guideline on school health services: Web Annex A. Compendium. ISBN 9789240030664.
Use: examples/context for school-health service intervention scope. The compendium lists 87 interventions identified as essential/suitable for SHS.

## G2 — Controlled Terminology and Concept Framework

| Thai controlled term | English | Operational use in textbook |
|---|---|---|
| การวัด | Measurement | assigning/obtaining data about an attribute according to a method; do not equate automatically with evaluation |
| การประเมิน | Assessment | systematic collection/interpretation of information to understand status/performance; distinguish from evaluative judgment |
| การประเมินโปรแกรม | Program evaluation | systematic examination of program processes/outcomes to support conclusions and decisions |
| การติดตาม | Monitoring | routine/ongoing observation of implementation or indicators; not synonymous with evaluation |
| ทฤษฎีโปรแกรม | Program theory | explanation of how/why program activities are expected to contribute to outcomes |
| แบบจำลองตรรกะ | Logic model | visual/narrative representation linking need, inputs, activities, outputs and outcomes |
| คำถามการประเมิน | Evaluation question | question that specifies what the evaluation needs to learn/judge |
| ตัวชี้วัด | Indicator | observable/measurable information used as evidence for a question or construct |
| ผลผลิต | Output | direct products/services/deliverables of activities |
| ผลลัพธ์ | Outcome | changes or effects associated with the program at specified levels/timeframes |
| ความตรง | Validity | degree to which evidence and interpretation support the intended use/construct; explain type/context before shorthand claims |
| ความเที่ยง | Reliability | consistency/precision of measurement under specified conditions |
| คุณภาพข้อมูล | Data quality | fitness of data for intended use, including completeness, accuracy/credibility, representativeness and bias considerations |
| การตรวจสอบแบบสามเส้า | Triangulation | deliberate use/comparison of multiple sources/methods/perspectives to strengthen or qualify interpretation |
| เกณฑ์การตัดสิน | Criterion/criteria | explicit basis/standard used to compare evidence before evaluative judgment |
| ข้อวินิจฉัยเชิงประเมิน | Evaluative judgment | reasoned conclusion reached by interpreting evidence in relation to criteria |
| ข้อเสนอแนะ | Recommendation | action-oriented proposal explicitly linked to findings/judgment |
| การประเมินซ้ำ | Re-evaluation | planned return to evidence after action/improvement to assess subsequent status/change |
| ผู้มีส่วนได้ส่วนเสีย/ผู้เกี่ยวข้อง | Interest holders / stakeholders | use context-sensitive language; when describing CDC 2024 preserve the source's current concept and explain terminology |

### Locked conceptual spine
Health Problem/Need → Program Theory/Logic Model → Objectives → Evaluation Questions → Indicators → Measurement & Instruments → Validity + Reliability → Data Collection → Analysis → Evidence → Interpretation → Compare with Criteria → Judgment → Recommendation → Action → Re-evaluation.

### Guardrails
- Output ≠ Outcome.
- Reliable ≠ Valid.
- High satisfaction ≠ program success by itself.
- Descriptive association ≠ causal effect.
- Conflicting-looking evidence may represent different constructs/outcomes.
- “ยังสรุปไม่ได้จากหลักฐานที่มี” is a legitimate evaluative conclusion when justified.
- Criteria precede defensible judgment.
- Recommendations must trace back to findings and acknowledge limitations.
