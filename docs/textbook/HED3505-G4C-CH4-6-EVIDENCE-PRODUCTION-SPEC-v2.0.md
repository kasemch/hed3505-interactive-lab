## 1. Evidence decisions locked
- Testing Standards (AERA/APA/NCME, 2014) is the principal standards reference for validity and reliability/precision. Validity is not a property label earned by one statistic; claims must concern the intended interpretation/use and supporting evidence.
- Lynn (1986) and Polit, Beck & Owen (2007) support historical/methodological treatment of content validity/CVI. They must not be presented as universal IOC rules.
- IOC is presented as a content-alignment procedure commonly used in Thai educational work. It is explicitly distinguished from CVI and from the broader validity argument. No universal “IOC ≥ X = valid instrument” rule is permitted without a source and context.
- CDC Program Evaluation Framework 2024 is the primary source for credible evidence, indicators, data quantity/quality, process/implementation evaluation and outcome evaluation.
- Process/implementation evaluation asks how implementation relates to intended theory of change and can address content, quantity, quality and structure. Outcome evaluation assesses whether intended outcomes were achieved; it does not by itself establish causality. Impact evaluation is the causal/counterfactual category.
- Triangulation is taught as systematic comparison/integration of multiple sources, methods, investigators or perspectives; convergence is not assumed and divergence is analytically meaningful.
