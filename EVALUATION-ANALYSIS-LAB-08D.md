# HED3505-EVALUATION-ANALYSIS-LAB-08D
## Certificate Branding Decision

Date: 20 September 2026
Status: APPROVED / IMPLEMENTED

## Decision
Do not include a university logo or seal on the HED3505 course-completion certificate.

## Result
- RU logo placeholder removed.
- Official-logo dependency removed.
- Certificate remains identified by department, faculty, university name, course code, instructor, learner data, completion date, and Certificate ID.
- No AI-generated or reconstructed university seal will be used.

## Boundary
The certificate remains a course learning-activity completion certificate, not an official university qualification or professional credential.

## Release impact
OFFICIAL BRANDING ASSET GATE: CLOSED / NOT REQUIRED
CERTIFICATE VISUAL: READY FOR FINAL DEVICE/PDF ACCEPTANCE
