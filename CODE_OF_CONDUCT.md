# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this educational open-source project respectful, inclusive, academically responsible, and free from harassment.

## Expected behaviour

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and constructively;
- welcome questions and differing levels of technical experience;
- critique ideas and code rather than individuals;
- protect learner privacy and avoid sharing personal or sensitive information;
- distinguish evidence from interpretation and simulated examples from authentic data;
- respect intellectual property, attribution, and licensing requirements;
- accept maintainer decisions about project scope, academic integrity, privacy, and safety.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized or degrading conduct, deliberate disclosure of private information, malicious technical activity, plagiarism, falsification of evidence, or repeated disruptive behaviour is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates these expectations and may restrict further participation when necessary to protect the project or its community.

Reports should be made privately to the maintainer when public disclosure would expose personal, sensitive, security, or safety information. Good-faith reports will be reviewed on their merits.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and to interactions undertaken while representing the project.
